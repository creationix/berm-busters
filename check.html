Device check: tests the four things only a real device can prove. The full-screen layout you pick here is also used by the game.

1 · Screen

Add this page to the Home Screen, open it from there, and look at all four edges. The red, green, blue and orange bars should touch the very edge of the glass. If a strip shows beyond a bar, try the other layouts and note which one fixes it and what colour the strip was.


      


      

        
2 · Tilt steering

        
Tap Enable, allow motion if asked, then hold the device in landscape and turn it like a steering wheel. The dot should follow: right when you turn right, and reach the end at about 30°.

3 · Gamepads and touch

        
Connect a controller and press any button on it; browsers hide gamepads until then. Move the left stick and press buttons to see them light up. Put several fingers on the screen to check multi-touch.

        

        

      


      

        
4 · Device-to-device connection

        
On one device tap Host. On every other device, on the same Wi-Fi, type the four letters and tap Join. Each connection should say "direct" with a round trip of a few milliseconds.
